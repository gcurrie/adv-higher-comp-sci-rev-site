📋About This Course

Advanced Higher Computing Science is assessed through two components: a question paper worth 55 marks and a project worth 80 marks — a total of 135 marks. Notice that this is the opposite balance to Higher: at Advanced Higher the coursework is worth more than the exam (roughly 60% project, 40% question paper), so the project deserves at least as much of your effort as exam revision.

The course has three areas of study — Software Design & Development, Database Design & Development and Web Design & Development — plus the integration of these technologies, which runs through both the question paper and the project. Analysis, testing and evaluation are common to all three areas and are covered together in the Integration section of this site.

This website covers: Integration (Analysis, Testing & Evaluation), Software Design & Development and Database Design & Development. Web Design & Development is only covered where DDD candidates need it — see the Web Integration page at the end of the DDD section.

📏Project Requirement Limits

From session 2025–26, the project has strict limits on its size. SQA markers apply a penalty to projects that exceed them, so scoping your project correctly is part of the assessment:

  • no more than six end-user requirements
  • four functional requirements that relate to Advanced Higher concepts
  • three functional requirements that relate to integration
  • between four and eight additional functional requirements, including at least two that relate to validation of keyboard input
