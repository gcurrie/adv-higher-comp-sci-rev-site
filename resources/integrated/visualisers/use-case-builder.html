THE SCENARIOQUICKBITE ONLINE TAKEAWAY

QuickBite is an online takeaway ordering system. Customers browse the menu and place orders. Placing an order always requires the payment to be authorised by an external payment provider. Customers may optionally add a tip for the driver. Order details are saved to the shop's database. Registered customers can do everything customers can, and can also reorder a saved favourite.

Exam tip When drawing a use case diagram from a scenario, work in this order: actors (anything outside the system, including the database), use cases (each action, inside the boundary), associations (every actor linked to at least one use case), then hunt the wording for “always” («include», arrow at the included use case), “optionally” («extend», arrow back at the base) and “can do everything X can” (generalisation, arrow at the ancestor).
