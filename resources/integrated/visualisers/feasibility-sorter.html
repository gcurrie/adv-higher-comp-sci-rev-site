Exam tip Naming the type is only half an answer. In the exam, tie it to the scenario and state the consequence: “Time feasibility — the system is needed before the new surgery opens in three months, and if it cannot be delivered by then the project should not go ahead.”

Watch for overlaps: many scenarios touch two types (a licence that costs too much is legal and economic). In an exam, pick the type the question's wording points at — and justify it from the scenario, never from a memorised definition alone.
