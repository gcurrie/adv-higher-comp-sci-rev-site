        Exam tip
        When a question says “using appropriate object-oriented terminology”, mark your own answer by counting the technical words in it. A three-mark description of one line of code should contain something close to: instantiates, object, class, constructor, parameters, instance variables. If your sentence would still make sense to someone who had never studied OO, it is almost certainly too vague to score.
      

      
    
      
    The commonest slips: saying class when you mean object; saying variable when you mean instance variable; and answering inheritance when the question is really about polymorphism.
