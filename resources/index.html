💡Revision Advice

  • Terminology first. Advanced Higher rewards precise vocabulary — instantiation, surrogate key, mandatory participation, integrative testing. Every topic page on this site opens with a Key Terms grid: learn those cold, and use them in every written answer.
  • Past papers beat notes. Around half the marks are 'C'-type and set in familiar contexts — the fastest gains come from working the short stand-alone questions at the start of each section under time pressure.
  • Practise reading code, not just writing it. "Read and explain" questions appear in both SDD and DDD — trace the code line by line and narrate it with correct terminology.
  • Don't neglect the project. It's 60% of your grade. Every hour of exam revision should be matched by progress on your project evidence.
