📊What's New at Advanced Higher (vs Higher)

Advanced Higher SDD builds directly on Higher programming. The key additions are:

TopicAdvanced Higher adds…
DesignUML class diagrams; wireframes now annotated with validation and underlying processes
Data structures2D arrays; arrays of objects; the operation of single and double linked lists (describe/exemplify only)
ConstructsThe full object-oriented toolkit: class, object, property, method, sub-class, encapsulation, inheritance, instantiation, polymorphism
DatabaseCode to open/close a database connection, execute SQL queries and format the results
AlgorithmsBinary search, bubble sort and insertion sort (replacing the three Higher algorithms) — including their efficiency
