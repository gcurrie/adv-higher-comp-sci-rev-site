📏The Rules That Catch People Out

  • The project must integrate two technologies — SDD (OO or procedural) with DDD, DDD with SDD or WDD, or WDD with DDD.
  • You must use one of SQA's four templates (on the SQA website — see Resources).
  • Requirement limits are enforced with a marker's penalty: max 6 end-user requirements; 4 AH-concept functional requirements; 3 integration functional requirements; 4–8 additional functional requirements including at least 2 for keyboard-input validation.
  • It's an open brief — agree your choice of problem with your teacher so it's achievable in the recommended 40 hours.
  • Once submitted, the project cannot be returned for further work — evidence as you go (screenshots, listings, test tables).
