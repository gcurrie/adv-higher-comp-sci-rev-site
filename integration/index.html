Integration — Analysis, Testing & Evaluation

Analysis, testing and evaluation apply to software, database and web development alike — and to solutions that integrate them. Master them once, apply them everywhere.

ℹ️
The integration of technologies is central to Advanced Higher. Your project must integrate two areas of the course (for example a program that reads from and writes to a database), and the question paper includes questions that draw on more than one area. The content on these pages applies to SDD, DDD and WDD — where something is specific to one area, it is clearly flagged.
